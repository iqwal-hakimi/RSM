About Rakan Sukarela Muda

Rakan Sukarela Muda (RSM) is a youth volunteer platform that encourages young people to contribute their time, skills, and energy towards community development and social activities.

Through volunteering, RSM aims to build responsible, caring, and active young leaders who can create a positive impact in society.

Our Vision

To create a generation of young volunteers who are responsible, compassionate, and committed to improving their communities.

Our Mission

To provide opportunities for youth to participate in community services, develop leadership skills, and support social causes.

Our Values

🤝 Volunteerism

Helping others through kindness and action.

🌱 Responsibility

Taking responsibility for society and the environment.

⭐ Leadership

Developing young leaders with positive attitudes.
