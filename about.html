About Rakan Sukarela Muda

Building a generation of responsible and caring young volunteers.

Our Story

Rakan Sukarela Muda (RSM) was created to encourage young people to actively participate in community service and social activities. Through volunteering, youth can develop leadership, teamwork, and communication skills while helping to create a better society.

Our Vision

To become a platform that inspires young people to contribute positively towards communities and society.

Our Mission

To provide volunteer opportunities that develop leadership, responsibility, and social awareness among youth.

Our Core Values

🤝 Volunteerism

Helping others through kindness and action.

🌱 Responsibility

Taking responsibility for community and environment.

⭐ Leadership

Developing confident young leaders.

Why Join RSM?

By joining RSM, young people can gain new experiences, meet new friends, improve skills, and make a meaningful impact on society.
